# Meta-awareness questions at the fence

**Seed (2026-10-27, verbatim):** add meta-awareness questions for zenborg fences (What value will I gain by going there? If you're already there, you can ask: How much time have I spent here already? Am I gaining any value by staying here?)

**Shape of it:** two moments, two questions. At the gate, before entry: *what value will I gain by going there?* Once inside (a pass already granted): *how much time have I spent here already? am I gaining any value by staying?* The second needs the time-on-site the fence already tracks (`get_site_minutes`).

**Open:** whether the questions replace or sit beside the current gate friction; whether the answer is recorded or just asked. Not shaped.
