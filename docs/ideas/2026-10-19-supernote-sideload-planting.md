# Supernote sideload as a planting surface

**Seed (2026-10-19):** plant the day by hand on e-ink instead of on the laptop, through a custom Android app sideloaded on the Supernote. Stock LAN export and OCR (supynote) don't quite do the job.

**Why it came up:** the practice-resilience analysis found that a planted sit happens 87% of the time, and that a sit the system plants on its own reads as an assignment. Choosing is the active ingredient. Planting on the laptop opens the screen where the pull lives, and on weekends the laptop stays closed.

**Open:** whether planning is even the lever, versus a context-stable habit anchor (see the same-day conversation). Not shaped.
